How a visit works

01

Show us where it hurts

Tap the sore spots on a body map and tell us how bad each one is. It takes about a minute, and you only do it once — we reuse your answers next time.

02

We suggest, you decide

Our assistant reads your assessment and offers treatments our bone setter has approved for your condition. Anything he has ruled out never appears.

03

Pick a time that is really free

You only ever see times when a therapist and a room are both available. Tap one and it is booked. Pay at the counter when you come in.
