What this document is

This is the rule table used by the HilotSpa booking assistant at Knead Wellness Spa. It is applied before the assistant is consulted, by the system itself:

  • A treatment marked CONTRAINDICATED for a condition is removed from the list of treatments the assistant is permitted to see for a client who reported that condition. The assistant cannot suggest it, because it is never shown it.
  • A treatment marked INDICATED is presented to the assistant as matched to that condition, together with the reason written beside it. The assistant repeats that reason to the client, word for word, when asked why a treatment was suggested.
  • A treatment with no rule for a condition may still be offered. Nothing rules it out, and nothing has reviewed it.

The rules below were drafted by the researchers from the establishment's own archived treatment records. They are proposals. Signing this sheet states that a practitioner has read them and accepts them as the establishment's protocol.
